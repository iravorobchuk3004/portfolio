(01) The idea

One app, three ways to help a pet.

Shelters are full, and people who want a pet rarely know which one fits their life. Hoomie asks a few quick questions about home, time and routine, then ranks shelter animals by how well they fit. People who can't adopt yet can support a specific pet's care, and anyone who finds a lost animal can report it in three steps.

Adopt
Quiz, match score, profile, application with a status timeline
Help
Donate to one pet's care and see exactly where it goes
Found a pet
Photo, place on a live map, what help is needed

(03) Built, not just drawn

A working app on a real phone.

I designed the screens, UI kit and mascots in Figma, then built the app myself. It runs on iOS and Android through Expo Go, with a live map, location, photo upload and haptics. Everything is saved on the device, so it works without a server.
